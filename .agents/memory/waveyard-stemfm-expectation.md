Waveyard should function as a Stem.fm replacement with extra features. Live stem swapping and isolation are core capabilities, and the active stem controls should expose both directly instead of hiding source swaps in a lower-level picker. Studio should be understandable and usable after a listening session. The user also wants TunePat/Apple Music considered as a future library source if it can be made fluid, or at minimum as a tool to back up and store their Apple Music library.

**Why:** the user explicitly described these as expected product behavior, not optional enhancements or preset content, asked for quick and visible Stem.fm-like swapping, and named Apple Music backup/library use as a future focus.

**How to apply:** Put clearly labelled isolate and donor-swap actions together on the active stems, and verify them on desktop and mobile. Prioritize reliable replacement and a clear session-to-Studio workflow. Keep the Apple Music source/backup workflow in scope. Verify real end-to-end behavior rather than relying on labels, explanations, or a build alone.
