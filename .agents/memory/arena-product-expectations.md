Arena should be a ChatGPT-like personal workspace with useful local capabilities and tools, including practical open-source alternatives to paid software where feasible. Prioritize real end-to-end utility over presets, surface copy, or claims of readiness.

**Why:** The user said previous passes felt like an empty shell and that they had not been able to use the product despite repeated claims that it was ready.

**How to apply:** Verify complete input-to-usable-output workflows, and distinguish truly local capabilities from cloud-backed features.
