---
name: Waveyard compact listener
description: Preserve one live audio session while exposing controls in the compact listening dock.
---

Add compact-only status and controls through the existing session experience and its transport/performance actions. Do not mount another deck to expose compact controls.

**Why:** The listening host keeps one session engine mounted across routes so navigation does not interrupt playback. A second deck would create a competing audio engine and duplicate state.

**How to apply:** Keep compact actions connected to the existing session state, and verify them on non-workspace routes at phone widths.
