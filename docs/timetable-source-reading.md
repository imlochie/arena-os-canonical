## AMBIGUITIES — imported as `needs_configuration`, not guessed

1. **Garage Downtime (17:00–19:30) overlaps Dinner (18:30–19:30).** The image
   shows both. Whether Dinner interrupts Garage Downtime, or they genuinely
   run concurrently, is not stated.
2. **Unexplained gap 12:00–12:20** between Life Skills & Home and Health &
   Movement. No activity is printed there.
3. **Sleep has no printed end time.** Wind Down ends "11:30+".
4. **Wednesday DoorDash Night is printed as 5:30–8:30**, which does not fit
   inside the Life Period band (15:20–17:00) and overlaps Garage Downtime and
   Dinner. The image states both; it does not reconcile them.
5. **AM/PM is not printed.** Read as 10:15 AM through 11:30 PM, which is the
   only ordering consistent with the row sequence — but this is an inference,
   recorded as such.

Per the Core Rules printed on the timetable itself — *"Follow the periods, not
the clock"* — these overlaps may well be intentional. The College surfaces them
rather than resolving them.
