Play these two first: 11-synced-close, then 14-control-dry. Same two sources, same arrangement decision — one stretched, one not. That A/B is the cleanest test of whether syncing is worth anything musically.
The 10 dry renders are not synced, on purpose.
All ten used stretch ×1.000. Rubato a cappella singing scores tempo confidence 0.132–0.245 against a 0.5 gate, so the planner withheld stretching every time. Correct fail-safe behaviour, not a bug — the dry pack shows honesty, not mashup competence. The gate was not lowered to make it look synced.
Known weakness: in 3 of the 5 synced cases the vocal is audible for only 13–21% of the runtime. A short vocal cameo over a long bed reads as a weak arrangement even when every individual decision is sound.
